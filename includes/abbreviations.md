*[A₃]: The face-centred cubic lattice (fcc): how greengrocers stack oranges. Its dual A₃* is bcc. η = 3/2.
*[A₄]: The four-dimensional lattice of integer points in ℤ⁵ with coordinate sum 0.
*[fcc]: Face-centred cubic lattice (A₃): how greengrocers stack oranges.
*[bcc]: Body-centred cubic lattice (A₃*), the dual of fcc.
*[D₄]: The 4D checkerboard: integer points (a, b, c, d) with an even coordinate sum. Densest lattice in 4D; η = 3.
*[D₅]: The 5D checkerboard: integer points with an even coordinate sum, the densest lattice in 5D. Its dual D₅* is ℤ⁵ ∪ (ℤ⁵ + ½·1). Both have η = 6.
*[E₆]: Exceptional 6D root lattice (72 roots), a section of E₈, the densest lattice in 6D. E₆* is its dual.
*[E₇]: Exceptional 7D root lattice (126 roots), a section of E₈, the densest lattice in 7D. E₇* is its dual.
*[E₈]: An exceptionally symmetric 8D lattice (240 roots), the densest sphere packing there. η = 2304.
*[Ψ-minimal]: A basis whose potential Ψ² is the smallest among all bases of the lattice.
*[isodual]: Similar to its own dual lattice (a rotated, rescaled copy of it).
*[flag]: The nested chain of lattices spanned by the first 1, 2, 3, … basis vectors: a line inside a plane inside a 3-space…
*[flags]: Nested chains of lattices spanned by the first 1, 2, 3, … basis vectors of a basis.
*[dual lattice]: All vectors whose inner product with every lattice vector is an integer. Its Gram matrix is the inverse.
*[two ends]: A flag's first step (one vector) and last step (a hyperplane). Duality swaps them.
*[staircase]: The squared Gram–Schmidt heights r₁, r₂, … of a basis, drawn as a row of bars.
